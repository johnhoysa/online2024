Projects

Here’s a collection of projects I’ve worked on over the years. Each project reflects a bit of what I've learned, built, and enjoyed along the way.

Expedia

HTML, CSS, TypeScript, Adobe Experience Manager

I maintained and optimized the Partner and Escapia marketing sites in Adobe Experience Manager (AEM), focusing on performance, usability, and authoring flexibility. I created the ui.frontend module for the Escapia site to help streamline the use of a shared component library across multiple AEM instances, collaborated on service pack updates, and enhanced grid systems for more dynamic content layouts. I also implemented event listeners to support analytics efforts with real-time user engagement data.

British American Tobacco

HTML, CSS, JS, Adobe Experience Manager, Magento

I worked with Adobe Experience Manager (AEM) and Magento to build accessible, high-performing websites for multinational markets. My work included developing reusable components and forms, integrating product and user data via GraphQL, and supporting localization efforts. I also created internal documentation and style guides to help ensure consistency and maintainability across teams and platforms.

Nescafé Dolce Gusto

HTML, CSS, JS, Magento

I worked with Magento to develop and enhance websites for Nescafé Dolce Gusto’s international markets. My contributions included building and refining category and product landing pages, resolving translation and layout issues, and installing and styling plugins to ensure a consistent and user-friendly experience across international markets.

MacSales

HTML, CSS, JS, Craft CMS

I led the redevelopment and integration of the eCommerce site into Craft CMS, ensuring consistency in design and brand messaging across the platform. I built single-page web applications for both the Used Macs Configurator and My Upgrades sections, leveraging in-house APIs for dynamic functionality. Additionally, I implemented a custom page builder using the Neo plugin, empowering the marketing team to quickly create and manage content with ease.

ESC 13

HTML, CSS, JS, ExpressionEngine CMS

At ESC 13 I built and maintained the organizations primary site and directed the web development project life cycle. One of my favorite projects was developing a video commenting web app for the video department. This tool significantly reduced the time required for video editing by streamlining feedback and collaboration. Another standout project was building an online conference platform. This platform featured registration, profile and networking capabilities, live video streaming, and user commenting, creating an engaging virtual experience.

The Parent Companion

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, ExpressionEngine CMS

During my time at ESC 13, I had the privilege of developing the Parent Companion site. It was created for parents to help register their children with diagnosed or suspected disabilities to Texas schools. What stands out most about this project is the incredible passion and commitment from the volunteers. Their personal stories resonated with our team, offering the insights we needed to create a successful and user-friendly site to help parents along their journey.

About
